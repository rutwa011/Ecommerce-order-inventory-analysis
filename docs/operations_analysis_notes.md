# Operations, Delivery & Returns Analysis

## Business Questions

1. Which warehouses process the highest order volume?
2. Which warehouses have the fastest delivery times?
3. What percentage of orders are delivered late?
4. Which warehouses have the highest late-delivery rates?
5. Which shipping methods are most reliable?
6. What percentage of orders are returned?
7. What are the most common return reasons?
8. Which products and categories have high return exposure?
9. Are late deliveries associated with higher return rates?
10. Are late deliveries associated with lower customer ratings?
11. Which warehouses perform best across delivery, returns, and ratings?
12. Is delivery performance improving over time?

## Important Analytical Notes

Return rates at product/category level represent association
with returned orders. An order may contain multiple products,
so this does not necessarily prove that a specific product
caused the return.

Relationships between delivery delays, returns, ratings, and
review sentiment should be interpreted as associations rather
than causal effects.
